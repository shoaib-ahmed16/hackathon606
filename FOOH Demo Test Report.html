Verdict

The fog replay case (#1) is close to demo-ready. Its numbers now match the guide's reference values: 5 flights watched, 656 connecting passengers, 2 diverted, 1 holding, 2 crew at risk, and severity scores of 66/36/36/18/10. The Ask assistant answers three of the four 3-minute-test questions from live data. Four problems would be visible to judges and need fixing first. The worst: the flight panel offers to divert an aircraft that has already diverted and landed, and one deadline counts down to the wrong alternate.

What works

  • Replay KPIs match the guide. Flights affected, connecting passengers, diverted/holding, crew at risk and the severity ranking all match Developer Guide §4.3.
  • The LVP rule is enforced everywhere. G9455's captain is not LVP-qualified, so holding is blocked, the flight is flagged, and Recovery parks it with the reason.
  • Unknown data is never shown as safe. AUH, DWC and MCT show "?" with "Ask station", and the engine keeps them as "verify first".
  • Ask (⌘K) answers questions 1–3 correctly and names its source view (e.g. v_crew_legality_ranking).
  • The replacement finder filters out standby crew who aren't LVP-qualified, with a plain reason for each.
  • Downstream impact is built: same-aircraft knock-on legs and each crew member's next duty and rest.
  • The recovery plan respects 12 arrivals/h and sets aside flights that can't be sequenced, with reasons.
  • The feed ignores stale messages: G9472 shows a POSITION (IGNORED_STALE) entry.
  • The API requires sign-in: anonymous calls get 401.
  • No errors since the redeploy: no HTTP errors, and no NaN, "undefined" or "Invalid Date" on screen.
  • Responses are fast: 150–330 ms typical; the slowest is /exceptions at about 640 ms.

Findings

Blocker

B1

The flight panel offers to divert aircraft that already diverted

Seen: G9464 landed at RKT at 02:04Z after an executed diversion (its own briefing shows DIVERT EXECUTED RKT). The decision area still says "No decision yet. The engine's top option is Divert to RKT", with a Propose: Divert to RKT button. The recommendations rank six alternates by fuel margin, plus "Keep holding", for an aircraft on blocks. There are two options numbered #1 (Divert RKT and Replace the crew), and "Earlier decisions (1)" contradicts "No decision yet".

Fix: once a diversion is executed, the decision area shows that decision and its status. The options switch to the at-alternate set: replace crew, commander discretion, or depart by the latest legal time. Number the options once.

Product guide §5.4 (decision area first), Developer guide §7.1

B2

One deadline counts down to a different alternate than the recommendation

Seen: for G9455 the engine recommends Divert to FJR, leaving the hold by 03:18Z. The deadline card and board counter instead show "Last moment to divert to RKT on fuel: 03:23Z". That gives the duty manager 5 minutes that don't exist for the recommended option.

Fix: take the deadline from the top executable option, or from the earliest leave-by time among executable options, and name that alternate.

Developer guide §7.2 DeadlineCard

B3

Stale ETAs drive crew legality

Seen: at 02:45Z, G9455 (ETA 02:10Z) and G9621 (ETA 02:15Z) still show "Airborne". The crew bar says "Lands 02:10Z", 35 minutes in the past, and the 110-minute margin at landing is computed from that past time. These crews look safer than they are.

Fix: projected landing = the later of the ETA and now plus remaining time. Mark the row "ETA overdue" and lower the confidence. Raise a verification item when ETA < now and the flight is still airborne.

Product guide §2.2 (missing or conflicting data means "verification required")

B4

Live cases still use the wrong clock

Seen: the LIVE scenario clock is 20 Nov 16:45Z, but case #5 holds 23 Nov arrivals. All 98 flights show crews with "57 h – 83 h left", "data 477 min old", every row in "Decide soon" with "?", and the KPI bar says 98 flights affected and 4,867 passengers affected, with no disruption at all. The header says "data 0 s ago" while every row says 477 min.

Fix: deploy the time-base rules (TB-1 to TB-5). Show "Duty starts HH:MMZ" for duties that haven't started. Don't count SCHEDULED flights as affected unless the hub is restricted. For the demo, open on case #1 or hide the LIVE cases.

H4

The Station view doesn't work on a phone

Seen: body has min-width: 1024px. At 375 px the page is laid out 1024 px wide, so the station buttons sit off-screen behind horizontal scroll. Checklist item 14 fails.

Fix: remove the min-width, and stack the Station view into one column with large tap targets below about 768 px.

DEMO_READINESS §4 flow 14, Developer guide §7.3

H5

Security of the hosted build

  • Plain HTTP on :8080, so the sign-in and bearer token travel unencrypted. There's nothing on 443.
  • The token is kept in localStorage, and the live stream sends it in the URL (/api/stream?token=…), so it lands in nginx access logs and browser history.
  • /ops-api/docs and /ops-api/openapi.json are public. They map all 80 endpoints, including a generic POST /api/records/{table}, DELETE /api/cases/{id} and POST /api/feed/push.
  • The generic table writer bypasses the "API is thin, functions only" contract. Deleting cases contradicts "never delete operational rows" (Developer guide §12).
  • The server version is shown (nginx/1.27.5). There's no Content-Security-Policy or X-Frame-Options header.
  • Before this update, the login page printed the demo users and their shared password. I didn't recheck it after the update, so please confirm whether it's still there.

Fix: put the app behind HTTPS on a hostname, and move the token to an HttpOnly cookie or a short-lived stream ticket. Restrict the docs to admins. Remove or role-lock records/{table} and case delete. Remove the password from the login page.

Medium

M1

Stand counts disagree

The header strip shows "RKT ✓ 4 stands", while the alternates table in the same panel shows RKT 2, after the two diverted aircraft. Show one figure: stands left.

M2

Station status contradicts itself

G9464 has every step ticked, including "Ready to depart", yet reads "OCC needs: confirm the aircraft is ready". Both RKT aircraft say "Waiting for arrival slot" while SHJ is open at 12/h and Recovery already slots G9588D at 03:20Z. Drive "waiting for" from the recovery plan.

M3

The next-deadline bar skips overdue deadlines

G9464's latest legal departure passed at 02:38Z (it sits in the "Act now" lane), but the header and KPI show G9472 in 27 min. Show overdue items first, in red, as "passed N min ago".

M4

Engine codes and raw IDs on screen

Examples: CREW_NOT_LVP_QUALIFIED_FOR_HUB and ENDURANCE_ENDS_BEFORE_REOPENING in the rationale; "Decision approved: crew_replace" and "Crew replacement: 108877 out, 109809 in" in the briefing; "disruption_case INSERT" in the live briefing; crew shown as "CC 106683" in downstream impact. Map codes to plain words and show names (or position + flight). DEMO_READINESS issue 7 is still open.

M5

Different screens use different clocks

The briefing shows scenario time (02:20Z), the Audit page shows server time (12:40Z) for the same events, and Data quality shows "raised 21 Nov 16:39" with no zone (it's SHJ local). Label each one, or show scenario time with the server time on hover.

M6

Polling load

Each open board re-fetches 17 endpoints about every 8 s, even though the /api/stream event stream is connected. /exceptions returns all 644 open exceptions (252 KB, not filtered by scenario) every time. That's about 2.5 MB per minute per browser. Refresh on stream events, poll slowly as a fallback, and filter exceptions by case.

M7

Deep links aren't live

The panel buttons read "Link template not configured yet", while Integrations lists templates (example.com placeholders). This is MVP story 10: either show the placeholder link or hide the buttons for the demo.

M8

Data oddities in live case #5

A five-digit flight number, G91831 (KTM→SHJ), and two flights with "no tail" (G9133, G9106). Check the loader for these rows.

Coverage and limits

  • Tested in a browser session you signed in to, on the build deployed during this session (after your update). Cases #1 (replay) and #5 (live) were checked in detail.
  • Read-only: no proposals, approvals, executions, what-if runs, live data pushes, reports or new cases. Flows 1, 2, 6, 7, 9–11 and 13 need a demo copy and your go-ahead.

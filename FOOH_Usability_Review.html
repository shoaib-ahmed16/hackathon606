Can OCC, Dispatch and Crew Control decide quickly?

Run 21 Nov 2026Roles: Dispatcher 1, Crew Controller 1, OCC Duty Manager (each signed in separately)Where: What-if case #6 (fog replay, SHJ closed 60 min longer)

Verdict

The core decision loop is easy. A diversion went from proposal to execution in about eight clicks across three roles. Every step showed the same "What will happen" preview, notifications took approvers straight to the flight, and nobody could approve outside their role. The rest of the app hasn't caught up with that loop. The home screen buries the flight list, the menus are crowded and identical for every role, the screen right after executing a diversion shows a misleading deadline, and four of the six items from the morning discussion are only partly built or missing.

The decision we ran

G9472 (MAA→SHJ) was holding with about 20 min of fuel, SHJ was closed until about 03:45Z, and the engine recommended diverting to RKT.

DISPATCH · 2 clicks · easy

Opened G9472, clicked Propose: Divert to RKT. The dialog listed what would happen: recovery flight G9472D created, crew legal end moving from 03:50Z to 03:05Z, return needing discretion or a replacement crew, RKT stands 4 → 3, 28 min spare fuel, 164 connecting passengers. It also listed who must approve. Clicked Propose decision, and a confirmation said approvers had been notified. Dispatch had no Approve button on its own proposal.

CREW CONTROL · 3 clicks · easy

The bell showed 1 unread: "G9472: DIVERT proposed, your approval is needed". Clicking it opened the flight. The board row showed "Needs your approval · 0/2". Review and approve as Crew Control showed the same preview plus "After your approval it still needs OCC". Approved, and the confirmation said "Waiting for OCC".

OCC · 4 clicks · some friction

Approvals → Review as OCC, which said "You are the last required approver". Approved, and the confirmation said "Approved by all required roles. OCC can execute". The queue then left OCC on an empty panel, so I had to find G9472 again, and the open drawer covered the Execute button. Execute showed the preview once more and a note that an aeroMart entry would be queued.

RESULT · confusing

The board and KPIs updated (1 diverted). The flight immediately read "At alternate / On ground at RKT", and the header said "Next deadline: passed 40 min ago, latest legal departure from RKT 02:13Z", a time before the diversion happened. The aeroMart line is a placeholder: the Integrations page shows a "Manual task" row, and nothing is sent to aeroMart.

What works well

  • One consequence preview, shown at every step. The proposer, each approver and the executor all see the same "What will happen" list.
  • Approvals that guide you. "After your approval it still needs OCC" and "You are the last required approver" say exactly where the decision stands.
  • Notifications take you to the flight, and the board row carries a "Needs your approval · 0/2" chip.
  • Role guardrails: Dispatch can't approve its own proposal, and multi-role approval is enforced.
  • Per-crew N and N+1 legality in Crew Control: each crew member's status, max FDP, time to spare or shortfall, and when they're legal for their next duty.
  • "Why this time?" explains a deadline in plain steps: endurance, flight time to the alternate, spare fuel, reopening, sign-in, sectors, FDP rule.
  • Alternate ranking from history: fuel margin uses P50/P90 leg times from historical flights, plus return block time and past diversion counts.

Usability findings

U1

Right after a diversion is executed, the screen says the aircraft is on the ground and a deadline passed 40 min ago

Seen: at 02:53Z, seconds after executing, G9472 reads "At alternate · On ground at RKT". The headline deadline is "Latest legal departure from RKT 02:13Z · passed 40 min ago". The flight to RKT takes about 15 min, and 02:13Z is before the decision was made. The duty manager's first reaction will be that something went wrong.

Fix: show "Diverting to RKT · ETA ~03:08Z" until landing. When the crew already can't return legally, say so in words: "Crew cannot fly RKT→SHJ legally. Options: commander discretion until 06:05Z, or replace crew." Don't show a departure time that already passed before the decision.

U2

After the final approval, OCC has to hunt for Execute

Seen: approving from the queue closes the dialog and leaves an empty panel. The Approvals drawer stays open and covers the flight panel, so the first click on "Execute decision" landed under it.

Fix: when the approver is also the executor, offer "Approve and execute" or an "Execute now" button in the confirmation. Close the drawer and open the flight after approving.

U3

On the home screen, the flight list starts below the first screen

Seen: the first screen is a two-row menu, 8 KPI tiles, two rows of filters and an 8-line briefing. The "Act now" lane starts at the bottom edge, and half the width is an empty "Select a flight" panel.

Fix: put the KPIs in one compact row, collapse the briefing to its newest 2 lines, and open the top "Act now" flight in the panel by default.

U4

Every role gets the same crowded menu

Seen: about 20 items in two rows: New case, Manage case (close or delete), Rules & filters, Integrations, Airport capacity, Audit, Data quality, Data explorer, What if, Push live data, Report, Data sources, and more. Dispatch and Crew Control see admin and developer tools, including case delete and Push live data.

Fix: show each role only its own actions. Put the rest under one "Tools" menu, and keep Data explorer, Data sources and Push live data for admins.

U5

The Dispatch tab is the OCC board

Seen: the Dispatch view is identical to OCC. A dispatcher's job is fuel and alternates across all airborne flights, and today that means opening flights one at a time.

Fix: a Dispatch table of airborne and holding flights with columns for fuel on board, endurance, fuel at SHJ and at the best alternate, the leave-the-hold-by time, and stands left.

U6

The header moves while it loads, and a click opened the hub-status form

Seen: as OCC, the Approvals count appeared a moment after the page loaded and pushed the header buttons sideways. A click meant for Approvals opened "Update SHJ (hub)", which can close or reopen the airport. I closed it without saving.

Fix: reserve space for badge counts, and move the hub-status editor away from the header actions (for example, behind an edit icon on the SHJ chip).

U7

Approvers don't see the urgency or the crew detail they need

Seen: the approvals queue lists "G9472 – Divert to RKT" with no countdown, although only 20 min remained. The Crew Control dialog says "Return needs commander discretion or a replacement crew" but doesn't name the crew, show the N+1 shortfall (45 min), or link to "Find replacement".

Fix: show the deadline countdown on each queue item. In the Crew Control dialog, show the crew with N and N+1 status, plus a "Find replacement" link.

U8

Engine codes and raw IDs where people read

Seen:

  • The proposal rationale is pre-filled with the engine's text, including ENDURANCE_ENDS_BEFORE_REOPENING, and that text goes to every team.
  • Dialogs and the queue show CREW_CONTROL · waiting, while the panel says "Crew Control".
  • The briefing shows "Crew replacement: 108877 out, 109809 in", and the crew panel shows FLIGHT_FEED_STALE.

Fix: pre-fill a short plain sentence ("Divert to RKT: fuel runs out before SHJ reopens; 28 min spare on arrival"), keep the engine detail behind "Why?", and use names everywhere.

U9

Timestamps don't match what happened

Both approvals and the execution are stamped 02:45Z, though they happened between 02:50Z and 02:53Z by the case clock. The notification shows 13:48Z and the outbox 13:53Z, which are server time. A duty manager reading the record can't reconstruct the order of events.

U10

The proposer can't withdraw or change a proposal

Once Dispatch proposes, nothing on Dispatch's screen lets them withdraw or replace it if the picture changes, for example if FJR becomes the better option.

U11

Tied options aren't explained

RKT and FJR both score 0.73. RKT is shown as #1 without saying why. FJR's leave-the-hold-by time is earlier (03:08Z vs 03:13Z), which matters to a dispatcher.

U12

The recommendation goes stale after execution

After executing, the decision area says "Next step: Replace the crew", but the recommendation card below still shows the old "DIVERT RKT" reasoning until it refreshes.

U13

What-if gives no feedback

"Run what-if" closes with no confirmation and leaves you on the original case, so you have to find the new case in the dropdown. In the copy, the briefing shows "SHJ OPEN – Fog lifting" next to the what-if closure, while the header says Closed.

U14

Accessibility

All 8 KPI tiles have the same accessible name, "Filter the board", so a screen reader can't tell them apart. The proposal, approval and execution dialogs aren't marked as dialogs. In Crew Control, clicking a flight number does nothing; only "Open" works.

Recommended order

  1. Fix the after-execution screen (U1) and offer Execute straight after the final approval (U2). These are the moments judges will watch.
  2. Build the FDP sign-in behaviour: show the bracket, a report-time control before sign-in, a lock after sign-in.
  3. Deploy the capacity endpoints so the Airport capacity page and the slot figures work.
  4. Add the fuel card (fuel on board, burn, fuel at landing, uplift for the return leg). Fuel on board is already in the API.
  5. Clean up the home screen and menus by role (U3–U5), and stop the header moving while it loads (U6).
  6. Plain language and one clock in rationales, chips, the briefing and timestamps (U8, U9).
  7. Add connection detail, and the flight plan and load sheet checklist for the return leg.
